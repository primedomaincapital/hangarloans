Where hangar financing actually happens.

Last checked: November 9, 2026

Hangar financing sits at the crossing of three things: the jets being delivered, the lenders who will and will not fund the building, and the fixed-base operators who earn from the roof space. The links below cover all three — the business aviation data that sizes demand, the finance guides that explain what lenders will fund, the construction guides that price the building, and the state programs that lend where the banks will not.

Demand: the fleet and the flights

McKinsey — The state of business aviation in 2026

McKinsey's first annual review of business aviation: record 3.9 million departures in 2025, the fractional fleet's growth, and hangar space named among the binding constraints on the industry.

FLYING — Business Aviation Demand is Outpacing Supply

Plain-English summary of the McKinsey findings, including the record departures, the delivery shortfall against the 2008 peak, and the pressure on hangar and parking space at leisure destinations.

AVweb — GAMA: Business Jets, Pistons Drive GA Growth in 2025

The General Aviation Manufacturers Association's 2025 shipment and billing report: 854 business jets delivered, up 11.8%, with airplane billings of $31.0 billion.

FAA — General Aviation and Part 135 Activity Survey, CY 2024

The FAA's annual survey of the general-aviation fleet: active aircraft, hours flown, and primary use, by aircraft type.

Alliance for Aviation Across America — General Aviation Economic Impact

Summary of the PricewaterhouseCoopers study: general aviation's $339.2 billion contribution to the United States economy in 2023.

Finance: what lenders will fund

AOPA Finance — When I finance a plane, may I finance a hangar with it?

AOPA's answer to the question every new owner asks: aircraft lenders use the airplane as collateral and cannot include a hangar in the same loan, with rare exceptions.

AOPA Finance — Aircraft Finance

AOPA's aircraft finance service: access to more than a dozen aircraft lenders, fixed or adjustable rates, and terms of 5 to 20 years.

Signature, a division of Esquire Bank — Aviation Banking

One of the few banks with a dedicated aviation desk: hangar construction and permanent financing, FBO improvement and working capital, and loans on leased land.

Build: what a hangar costs

Cascading Falls — Building the Nest: Aircraft Hangar Construction

Planning and budgeting guide for hangar construction: $60 to $120 per square foot, with a 15,000-square-foot hangar at $900,000 to $1,800,000.

CNSS Prefab Buildings — How Much Does an Aircraft Hangar Cost in 2026?

2026 hangar cost guide: turnkey $65 to $150 per square foot, shell-only $25 to $45, with costs by footprint from 1,500 to 30,000-plus square feet.

JRH Engineering — TX Aircraft Hangar Design: Sizes, Costs & Layout 2026

Licensed structural engineering guide to hangar design and 2026 costs: basic construction roughly $50 to $150 per square foot, higher in Florida for wind-rated structures.

Dataintelo — Private Hangar Construction Market

Market report on private hangar construction: T-hangars at $42 to $68 per square foot, box hangars at $58 to $94, with market shares by hangar type.

FBOs and state programs

PW Consulting — Worldwide Fixed Base Operator (FBO) Market 2026

FBO market research: $18.45 billion in 2025, projected to reach $25.31 billion by 2032, with hangarage and parking at about 18% of revenue.

Dataintelo — Fixed Base Operator Services Market

FBO services market report: $33.6 billion in 2025, projected to reach $61.2 billion by 2034, with more than 3,100 FBO locations in North America.

Nebraska DOT — Revolving Hangar Loan Program

Nebraska's interest-free hangar loan program for public-use airports: up to 80% of eligible costs, $1,000,000 maximum per project, 20-year repayment.

Caltrans — Airport Loans, Local Airport Loan Program

California's state loan program for public airports: loans for hangars, terminals, and fueling facilities, with a maximum term of 17 years.

NBAA — National Business Aviation Association

The business aviation industry association: advocacy, airport access, and the operating guidance that shapes where jets fly and where they are based.

Sky Harbour Group's model for building private hangar campuses at scale: 22 ground leases, a pipeline targeting roughly 200 airports, triple-net leases, and rents from the low $20s per square foot in Texas to about $80 in San Jose.

AInvest — Sky Harbour's Cash Flow Milestone Is Real. The Dividend Isn't Coming.

Sky Harbour's first quarter of positive consolidated operating cash flow in Q2 2026 — revenue of about $9.86 million, up roughly 50% — and the capital intensity still ahead: $318 million in equity raised since 2021 and $234 million in planned 2026 capital spending.

DCF Analyst — Sky Harbour Group Corporation (SKYH): Company Overview & Business Model
