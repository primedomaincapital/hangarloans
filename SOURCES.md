# Market-data and resources pages (November 8, 2026)

## market-data.html — figure sources (all figures seen in public page text)

- GAMA 2025 Aircraft Shipment and Billing Report, as reported February 2026 (AvBuyer; AVweb): 854 business jets in 2025, up 11.8% from 764 in 2024, strongest since 2009; airplane billings $31.0 billion, up 16.1% from $26.7 billion in 2024; total airplane deliveries 3,230, up 2.2% from 3,162; piston deliveries 1,782; turboprops 594; total aircraft deliveries (airplanes + helicopters) $35.7 billion, up 14.6%. GAMA President and CEO James Viola quote. https://www.avbuyer.com/index.php/articles/business-aviation-market-analysis/new-jet-shipments-spike-business-turboprops-flat-in-2025-114322 and https://avweb.com/aviation-news/gama-2025-aircraft-shipments-billings-report/
- McKinsey, "The state of business aviation in 2026: Resilient, despite headwinds" (2026): business aviation departures 3.9 million in 2025 (record), growing 4.9% a year since 2019; flight hours 6.4 million in 2025 vs 4.7 million in 2019; fractional fleet 990 aircraft in 2019 to 1,510 in 2025; fractional share of business jet flight hours 14% to 20%; leisure departures up 5.4% a year vs 2.1% at business centers; 326,000 of 417,000 additional U.S. departures at leisure airports; Patrick Gallagher (President, NetJets Aviation): infrastructure "is by far the biggest challenge we contend with." https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/aerospace-and-defense/our-insights/the-state-of-business-aviation-in-2026-resilient-despite-headwinds
- FLYING, "Business Aviation Demand is Outpacing Supply — What It Means for 2027" (McKinsey findings): 842 business jet deliveries in 2025 (McKinsey count) vs 1,290 peak in 2008; retirements about 110 a year in 2025 vs about 300 in 2019; average aircraft flew 19% more hours in 2025 than in 2019. https://www.flyingmag.com/mckinsey-business-aviation-demand-supply-2027/
- PW Consulting, "Worldwide Fixed Base Operator (FBO) Market 2026": $18.45 billion in 2025, $25.31 billion by 2032, CAGR 4.62%; business aviation 74.9% of the market; fueling 60.1% of revenue; hangarage and parking 18.1%. https://pmarketresearch.com/worldwide-fixed-base-operator-fbo-market-research/
- Dataintelo, "Fixed Base Operator Services Market": $33.6 billion in 2025, $61.2 billion by 2034, CAGR 6.9%; North America 44.1% share; more than 3,100 active FBO locations in North America by early 2026; Signature Aviation and Atlantic Aviation lead. https://dataintelo.com/report/fixed-base-operator-services-market
- Dataintelo, "Private Hangar Construction Market": T-hangars $42–$68 per sq ft (34.2% share, about $4.86 billion in 2025); box hangars $58–$94 per sq ft (28.6% share, about $4.06 billion). https://dataintelo.com/report/private-hangar-construction-market
- Cascading Falls, "Building the Nest: Aircraft Hangar Construction" (December 29, 2025): $60–$120 per sq ft; 15,000 sq ft hangar $900,000–$1,800,000; large facilities $2 million to $8 million. https://www.cascadingfallsinc.com/aircraft-hangar-construction
- CNSS Prefab Buildings, "How Much Does an Aircraft Hangar Cost in 2026?" (August 2026): turnkey $65–$150 per sq ft; shell-only $25–$45 per sq ft. https://www.cnssprefabbuildings.com/info/aircraft-hangar-cost-103631975.html
- JRH Engineering, "TX Aircraft Hangar Design: Sizes, Costs &amp; Layout 2026" (September 2026): basic construction roughly $50–$150 per sq ft; Florida higher for wind-rated structures; site work separate. https://www.jrhengineering.net/post/tx-aircraft-hangar-design-sizes-layouts-amenities-costs
- Nebraska DOT Division of Aeronautics, Revolving Hangar Loan Program (Commission approved May 8, 2026): loans up to 80% of eligible costs, maximum $1,000,000 per project, 20-year repayment, interest-free; revolving fund established 1992. https://dot.nebraska.gov/media/4yvlfcnb/revolving-hangar-loan-program-may-8-2026-commission-approved.pdf
- Caltrans, Airport Loans — Local Airport Loan Program: maximum loan term 17 years; rates set at the latest California General Obligation Bond sale rate; state loans for hangars, GA terminals, and fueling facilities. https://dot.ca.gov/programs/aeronautics/airport-loans
- AOPA Finance, "Financing Experimental Aircraft": maximum loan term 20 years, rates 4.99%–6.9%, minimum down payment 15%. https://finance.aopa.org/aviation-finance/types-of-finance/experimental-aircraft
- AOPA Finance, "Aircraft Finance": fixed or adjustable rates, 5–20 year terms, access to more than a dozen aircraft lenders. https://finance.aopa.org/aircraft
- AOPA Finance, "When I finance a plane, may I finance a hangar with it?" (January 2023): aircraft lenders use the aircraft as collateral and are unable to include hangar financing; rare exceptions. https://finance.aopa.org/resources/2023/january/20/when-i-finance-a-plane-may-i-finance-a-hangar-with-it
- Piston Aircraft Loans, rates as of November 2026 (citing AOPA): piston range 6.99%–9.99%; AOPA reported rates in the low 6% range in January 2026. Secondary citation — the AOPA January 2026 article itself was not fetched verbatim. https://pistonaircraftloans.com/
- FAA, General Aviation and Part 135 Activity Survey, CY 2024, as summarised by General Aviation News (February 11, 2026): 213,756 active aircraft in 2024; more than 29 million flight hours, up about 2% from 28.43 million in 2023. https://www.faa.gov/data_research/aviation_data_statistics/general_aviation/cy2024 and https://generalaviationnews.com/2026/02/11/ga-doing-more-with-less/
- Alliance for Aviation Across America, Executive Summary (PwC study for GAMA, February 2025): general aviation contributed $339.2 billion to the U.S. economy in 2023 and supported 1.3 million jobs. https://aviationacrossamerica.org/economic-impact/executive-summary/
- Signature, a division of Esquire Bank, Aviation Banking: hangar construction and permanent financing, FBO improvement and working capital, loans on leased land; Nick Martino (Vice President of Operations, CloudNine, Camarillo, CA) testimonial; luxury executive hangar with 255,000 sq ft of ramp space. https://signature.esquirebank.com/aviation-banking/
- City of Fayetteville, Arkansas, Resolution 109-06 (June 6, 2006): two $250,000 state grants, $700,000 city loan at 5% over 15 years for two corporate hangars at Drake Field; about $7,600 monthly hangar revenue; all hangars full with a waiting list. https://documents.fayetteville-ar.gov/WebLink/DocView.aspx?id=5737133&amp;dbid=0&amp;repo=COF
